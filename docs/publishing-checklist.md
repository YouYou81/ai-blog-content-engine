# Publishing Checklist

Use this checklist before creating a draft or uploading a cover image.

## Metadata

- Title includes the primary keyword.
- Title is 50-70 characters when the target language makes that practical.
- Meta Description is 140-160 characters.
- Keywords include primary keyword and 3-5 related long-tail keywords.
- Cover Image Alt is descriptive and not keyword-stuffed.

## Article Structure

- TDK is separate from the article body.
- TL;DR uses short bullet points.
- Introduction gives the conclusion first.
- Body includes practical examples, not only definitions.
- Comparison table appears only when it helps.
- Conclusion includes the intended CTA.
- FAQ has 3-5 plain questions and answers.
- FAQ is the final article section.

## GEO / AEO Readiness

- Each important section answers one clear search intent.
- Definitions are concise enough for answer engines.
- Claims are supported by authoritative links.
- FAQ questions match real user questions.
- The article can be quoted in AI answers without extra context.

## SEO

- Primary keyword appears naturally.
- Related long-tail keywords are distributed across headings and body.
- Internal links point to relevant pages.
- External links point to authoritative sources.
- Anchors describe the linked concept.

## Compliance

- The article emphasizes lawful and responsible use when discussing automation, scraping, CAPTCHA, data extraction, proxies, or security workflows.
- The article does not encourage private, restricted, sensitive, or unauthorized data access.
- The article avoids unsupported technical claims.

## MCP Publish

- Slug is final before publishing.
- `create_post` runs before `upload_image`.
- Cover image alt text matches metadata.
- Uploaded image URL is attached to the draft when supported.
- Draft ID, slug, image URL, and admin URL are recorded.
